Excellence Saccos is a member-owned savings and credit cooperative serving residents of Hawassa City. We offer both interest-based and interest-free financial services, built on transparency, integrity and a strong culture of saving.

Become a member Our objectives

Our vision

To become a preferred, reliable and leading Saving and Credit Cooperative Society (SACCO) in our country, built on member participation, transparency, integrity and innovation by fostering a strong culture of saving.

Our mission

To empower members toward sustainable economic prosperity by promoting a culture of saving, providing accessible, fair and quality saving and credit services, using modern technology, and upholding transparency, accountability and good governance.

Our objectives

  • Provide regular and voluntary savings and credit services based on members' needs, and build a strong financial institution by growing our investment capacity.
  • Offer both interest-based and interest-free financial services, centered on members.
  • Provide micro-insurance or Takaful services to protect members' savings and the loans we disburse, preventing social crises.
  • Advance financial inclusion and members' self-reliance by building the community's financial literacy and saving culture.
  • Resolve financial shortage and excess liquidity by creating financial linkages between financial institutions and the cooperative.

What we offer

Regular savings

Save steadily every month. The minimum is 50 birr a day (1,500 birr a month), or 10% of your net income.

Voluntary savings

Save extra beyond your regular savings, with interest-based or interest-free options such as Qard Hasan, Wadiah and Damaan.

Children's savings

A trust savings account that families open for children under 18.

Interest-free services

Sharia-compliant savings and financing with no interest paid or charged, with a focus on Ijara.

Interest-based services

Savings that earn interest and loans with interest, for members who prefer conventional services.

Micro-insurance and Takaful

Small-scale insurance that protects members' savings and loans, funded by contributions from members.

Safekeeping

We hold money and property in trust for our members.

How to join

  1. Fill in and sign the membership application form at our office.
  2. The management committee replies in writing within 3 days.
  3. Pay the one-time registration fee: 2,000 birr for an individual, 5,000 birr for an organization.
  4. Buy your starting shares within 3 months of approval. Each share costs 2,000 birr, and you need at least 5 shares. No member may hold more than 10% of all shares.
  5. Start your regular savings.

Figures are taken from our bylaw. Please confirm current amounts at our office before you apply.
